Product Requirements Document (PRD): Ali-A: 1 of 1 (999 Ping Edition)
1. Overview & Goal
Title: Ali-A: 1 of 1 — 22nd Birthday Edition

Target User: Ali-A (turning 22), playing via mobile Safari/Chrome from an Instagram DM link or on desktop.

Tech Stack: Single self-contained index.html (HTML5 Canvas + Vanilla JS + Web Audio API synthesized chiptune SFX/BGM). Zero external assets or build steps required.

Aesthetic: Midnight Y2K Coquette-Grunge (#120F17 deep charcoal-purple backdrop, #F7A8C4 baby pink, #C92A42 crimson rose, #D8DCE6 chrome silver, and #FF3B30 Valorant "High Ping / Packet Loss" warning red).

2. Visual Identity & Characters
Player Sprite: Pixel-art Ali-A (long dark hair, black lace-up crop top and shorts or rose-and-skull zip-up) accompanied by a floating pink winged bat/axolotl plushie sidekick.

HUD (Valorant + Birthday Hybrid):

Top-left: Health hearts (3 pixel hearts) + Score counter.

Top-right: Live fake Ping Counter fluctuating between 180ms and 999ms with a red Wi-Fi / Packet Loss warning icon.

3. Core Gameplay Loop (3 Stages, ~2.5 Minutes Total)
Stage 1: Midnight Lobby (Intro Screen)

Pixel-art bedroom/setup with an LED glow, mirror, and plushie on the bed.

Dialogue popup: "Match Found: Birthday Queue (999ms Ping). Gather your drops and survive the lag spikes to reach the 22nd Birthday Finale."

Controls: Desktop (A/D or Arrow Keys to move, W/Space/Up to jump + double-jump with bat wings, E or Click to throw an Initiator Recon/Flash orb) + responsive on-screen mobile touch buttons.

Stage 2: High-Ping Platformer ("Site Execute on 999ms")

Signature Mechanic — Lag Spikes: Every 10–12 seconds, a "NETWORK PROBLEM // 999 PING" banner flashes for 1.5 seconds with brief rubber-banding/screen-jitter visual effects (telegraphed fairly so it's funny, not frustrating).

Initiator Ability (Recon/Flash Pulse): Pressing E / F (or tapping the FLASH touch button) sends out a pink-and-crimson sonar wave that reveals hidden invisible platforms and stuns approaching hazards.

Collectibles:

Pink Winged Plushies (+150 pts)

Crimson Roses & Silver Chrome Hearts (+100 pts)

Radianite / Spike Birthday Orbs (+250 pts)

Hazards (Stunnable with Initiator Flash):

Floating "PACKET LOSS 100%" red boxes

"BLINDED" flash orbs

Rolling "999ms" lag spikes

Stage 3: Custom Cake & "1 of 1" Finale

Reaching the glowing vanity mirror portal transitions to an interactive Cake Decorator screen.

Ali-A clicks/taps buttons to customize her 22nd birthday cake: Frosting color (Midnight Black, Baby Pink, Crimson), Toppings (Red Roses, Silver Chrome Hearts, Mini Winged Plushie topper), and 22 sparkler candles.

Clicking "Light Candles & Lock In" triggers a chiptune birthday melody, pink/crimson confetti particles, and the final card: "HAPPY 22ND BIRTHDAY ALI-A — 1 OF 1".
